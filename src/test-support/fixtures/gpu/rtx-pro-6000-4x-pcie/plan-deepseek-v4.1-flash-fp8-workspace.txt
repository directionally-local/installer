Caveats
  - No official recipe covers RTX PRO Blackwell (sm120) GPUs: the vLLM recipe lists H100, H200, B200, GB200, B300, GB300 and AMD. vLLM's SM120 fix for this model (#60762, 64-token kernel blocks, merged 2026-11-05) is in no final release yet (v0.34.0rc2 only), and issue #59203 reports indexer_kv_dtype mxfp4 rejected on SM120 on the release tag. Expect to need a newer image.
  - The recipe's vram_minimum_gb is 614 with the Engram tables on the GPU; with them in host RAM the resident weights are ~286 GiB. Measure before relying on long contexts: the recipe says "measure before assuming a given context/batch fits".

Result    fits: GPU memory, host RAM, disk and driver
